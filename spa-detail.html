Luxury Aromatherapy Massage

Indulge in the ultimate relaxation experience with our signature aromatherapy massage.

Duration: 90 minutes

Price: $120

Description

Our Luxury Aromatherapy Massage combines the healing power of touch with the therapeutic benefits of essential oils. This treatment is designed to relieve stress, improve circulation, and promote overall well-being.

Benefits

  • Reduces stress and anxiety
  • Improves sleep quality
  • Boosts immune system
  • Relieves muscle tension and pain
  • Enhances mood and mental clarity

What to Expect

During your aromatherapy massage, our skilled therapist will use a blend of essential oils tailored to your needs. The massage combines various techniques, including Swedish and deep tissue, to provide a truly personalized experience.

You'll be asked to choose from a selection of aromatherapy blends, each designed to address specific concerns such as relaxation, energizing, or detoxification.

Preparation Tips

  • Arrive 15 minutes before your appointment to complete any necessary paperwork and relax.
  • Inform your therapist of any medical conditions, injuries, or areas of concern.
  • Avoid heavy meals before your massage.
  • Stay hydrated before and after your treatment.
